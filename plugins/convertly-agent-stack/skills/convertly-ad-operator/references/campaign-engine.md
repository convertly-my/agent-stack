# Convertly campaign engine

Use this loop for end-to-end campaign work. Skip stages only when the requested outcome clearly does not need them.

## 1. Evidence map

Build a compact evidence map from the narrowest Convertly tools:

- commercial truth: delivered revenue, TrueProfit, delivered CPA, refunds or returns, stock, cashflow, and budget caps;
- funnel truth: visitors, conversion rate, checkout or order friction, salespage promise, offer, price, proof, objections, and CTA;
- media truth: spend, attributed orders, campaign and ad concentration, fatigue, placement or audience patterns, and creative DNA;
- customer truth: approved voice-of-customer patterns, desired outcomes, anxieties, failed alternatives, buying triggers, and objections;
- uncertainty: missing tracking, weak sample size, stale evidence, confounding variables, or unsupported claims.

Label each item as fact, observation, inference, or hypothesis. Never turn missing data into a negative result.

## 2. Constraint and opportunity

Identify the single dominant constraint. Typical classes are tracking, traffic quality, creative resonance, offer strength, salespage conversion, unit economics, fulfilment quality, inventory, or cashflow.

Rank candidate actions by expected impact, evidence strength, speed to learn, cost to test, reversibility, and downside. Recommend the highest-leverage action, not the most elaborate one.

## 3. Strategic hypothesis

Write one falsifiable statement:

`For [persona + awareness], [message/offer/creative change] will improve [primary commercial metric] because [mechanism supported by evidence].`

Include the evidence, assumption, one changed variable, success threshold, kill threshold, minimum evidence, guardrail metrics, and what will be learned whether it wins or loses.

## 4. Angle portfolio

Create genuinely distinct belief shifts, such as:

- problem reframe;
- unique mechanism;
- proof or demonstration;
- objection reversal;
- identity or aspiration;
- comparison or failed alternative;
- offer/value stack;
- urgency grounded in a real constraint.

Score each angle for evidence fit, novelty, buyer relevance, proof availability, funnel fit, and policy or claim risk. Select the strongest testable angle. Keep runner-up angles as the next learning queue, not simultaneous noise.

## 5. Creative system

For each selected angle, specify:

- target persona, awareness stage, and desired belief shift;
- first-frame hook and visual stopping device;
- body structure, product mechanism, proof device, objection handling, offer, and CTA;
- format, aspect ratio, composition, talent or product direction, overlays, brand cues, and prohibited claims;
- primary text, headline, description, and matching landing-page message;
- the exact variable being tested and everything held constant.

Use native image generation only after the concept is approved or the user explicitly requests generation. Inspect generated output for claim accuracy, legibility, product fidelity, and placement-safe composition before upload.

## 6. Launch packet

Prepare exact campaign, ad-set, creative, and ad settings. Include objective, conversion location, optimization event, destination, attribution and URL tracking, audience and exclusions, placements, schedule, budget cap, naming, asset IDs, success rule, kill rule, and rollback plan.

Create objects paused whenever possible. Before active delivery or spend, show the final account, settings, assets, and budget and obtain immediate explicit confirmation.

## 7. Learning loop

Read results only after the minimum evidence or a hard safety guardrail is reached. Diagnose in this order:

1. tracking integrity;
2. delivered commercial outcome;
3. unit economics and customer quality;
4. funnel conversion;
5. creative and audience signal;
6. platform diagnostics.

Classify the result as scale, iterate, hold, stop, or inconclusive. Record the hypothesis, experiment, outcome, confidence, and merchant feedback in Convertly when write scope is available and the required confirmation has been obtained.

## 8. Scaling loop

Scaling is a new experiment, not a reward for a winning screenshot. Use the scaling constitution and readiness tool. Protect tracking, delivered TrueProfit, customer quality, inventory, cashflow, creative depth, cooldown, and portfolio concentration. Show the exact increment and rollback threshold before the official Meta write.
